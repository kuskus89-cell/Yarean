Our Story

Yarean is a recipe discovery platform designed for East African households. It helps users find meals they can prepare using the ingredients they already have, focusing on Kenyan and Somali cuisines that are affordable, practical, and culturally familiar.

The system prioritizes recipes that fully match the users available ingredients. When exact matches are not available, it suggests recipes that require minimal additional ingredients, which are clearly labeled as optional.
